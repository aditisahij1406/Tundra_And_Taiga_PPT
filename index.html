01 · THE PEOPLE

PASTORALISTS

WHO ARE THE PASTORALISTS?

Pastoralists are people who depend partly or largely on herding and raising animals for their livelihood. In the cold northern regions, communities have adapted their way of life to the harsh climate and seasonal changes.

WHERE DO THEY LIVE?

Pastoral communities live across parts of the Arctic tundra, forest-tundra and taiga of northern Eurasia and North America.

HOW DO THEY SURVIVE THE COLD?

Pastoralists move or manage their herds according to seasonal conditions and available food, using animals that are adapted to extreme cold.
